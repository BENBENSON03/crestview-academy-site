Welcome to Our School

Building Bright Futures Since 1995

Welcome to Crestview Academy. We are glad that parents, students and visitors have taken the time to get to know us. Our commitment is simple: to provide quality education, support each child's personal development, and help every student discover what they can achieve.

Since our founding, we have grown into a community built on a caring, structured learning environment. Our mission is to nurture curious, confident and responsible young people through a balanced approach to academics, character and creativity, with teachers, families and students working together.
