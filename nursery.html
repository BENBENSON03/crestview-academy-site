Crestview Nursery

A joyful, caring start to learning

Our nursery is a warm, play-filled place where little ones feel safe, curious and confident. Through stories, songs, games and gentle guidance, we help every child fall in love with learning.

Welcome

Welcome to Our Nursery

The early years are when children learn fastest, and we treat them with the care they deserve. Our classrooms are bright, calm and full of things to touch, build, sing and explore.

We follow a gentle, play-led approach: children choose activities, teachers join in and ask the right questions, and learning grows naturally from curiosity. We work as partners with families so that every child feels known and valued.

  • Safe and nurturing. Familiar faces, kind routines and clear boundaries.
  • Learning through exploration. Hands-on activities that follow each child's interests.
  • Partnership with parents. Regular, friendly updates on how your child is growing.

Early Learning

What Children Learn

Five key areas of development, taught through play so that learning always feels like fun.

  • Literacy

    Listening to stories, recognising letters and sounds, and enjoying the first steps of reading and mark-making.

    Rhymes · Letter sounds · Story time

  • Numeracy

    Counting, sorting, comparing shapes and spotting patterns using blocks, beads and everyday objects.

    Counting · Shapes · Patterns

  • Creativity

    Painting, drawing, modelling and pretend play that let children imagine, invent and express themselves.

    Painting · Clay · Role play

  • Communication

    Building vocabulary, speaking in sentences, listening carefully and sharing ideas with friends and adults.

    Talking · Listening · Songs

  • Social Skills

    Taking turns, sharing, showing kindness and managing big feelings while making friends.

    Sharing · Teamwork · Kindness

Learning Through Play

Play Is How Children Learn Best

Every activity is planned with a purpose, and every child gets time to explore at their own pace.

  • Educational Games

    Puzzles, building blocks and matching games that build problem-solving, counting and concentration.

  • Storytelling

    Picture books, puppets and acting out tales grow imagination, vocabulary and a lasting love of reading.

  • Arts and Crafts

    Painting, collage and clay help little hands grow strong while ideas take shape.

  • Music and Movement

    Singing, rhythm and dancing build listening skills, coordination and confidence.

  • Outdoor Activities

    Fresh air, running, climbing and nature walks keep bodies healthy and minds curious.

Our Environment

A Space Made for Little Learners

  • Bright Classrooms

    Light, airy rooms with child-height shelves, cosy reading corners and clear areas for different kinds of play.

Daily Routine

A Day in Our Nursery

A predictable rhythm helps young children feel secure. Here is a typical day, mixing active play, calm moments and plenty of fun.

Sample schedule only. Times will be confirmed by the school.

  1. 8:30 AM

    Welcome and Free Play

    A warm greeting, then children settle in with puzzles, books and building toys.

  2. 9:15 AM

    Circle Time

    Songs, greetings and a look at the day ahead, sitting together on the carpet.

  3. 10:00 AM

    Learning Centres

    Letters, numbers and early science explored through hands-on, play-based activities.

  4. 10:45 AM

    Handwashing and Snack

    A healthy snack shared with friends, practising good manners and independence.

  5. 11:15 AM

    Outdoor Play

    Running, climbing, ball games and exploring nature in the fresh air.

  6. 12:00 PM

    Story and Quiet Time

    Calm stories, soft music and a chance to rest after a busy morning.

  7. 12:45 PM

    Creative Arts

    Painting, clay, collage or music-making, with every creation celebrated.

  8. 1:30 PM

    Reflection and Home Time

    Children share the best part of their day, then head home with a smile.
