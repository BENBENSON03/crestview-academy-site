Welcome

Welcome to Our Library

A library is more than a room of books. It is where curiosity is fed, where reading becomes a habit and where pupils learn to find things out for themselves. Our library supports learning at every stage of school life, from a first picture book to independent research for examinations.

  • NurseryPicture books, rhymes and shared story time that spark a love of reading.
  • PrimaryStorybooks, early non-fiction and reading habits that build confident readers.
  • SecondaryReference, literature and revision resources for deeper study and exam preparation.

Collections

Resources and Collections

The kinds of material a school library offers. The school will confirm exactly what is available on its shelves.

  • Storybooks

    Illustrated stories and early readers that make reading fun for young learners.

  • Fiction

    Novels, short stories and adventure that grow imagination and vocabulary.

  • Non-Fiction

    Fact-based books on people, places, nature, technology and the world around us.

  • Dictionaries and Encyclopedias

    Reference works for checking words, facts and ideas while studying.

  • Science and Maths

    Books that explain how things work and make numbers and experiments easier to grasp.

  • History and Geography

    Atlases, histories and cultural studies that connect pupils to the wider world.

  • Exam Preparation

    Revision guides and practice material to support students ahead of examinations.

  • Digital Resources

    E-books and online learning tools, where the school provides them.

    Activities

    Activities and Reading Programmes

    Ways a library can bring reading to life. These are examples, and the school will confirm which programmes it runs.

    • Reading Clubs

      Small groups that read, share and talk about books together.

    • Storytelling Sessions

      Story time that builds listening, imagination and love of language.

    • Book Reviews

      Pupils write and share short reviews to recommend books to friends.

    • Reading Competitions

      Friendly challenges that reward curiosity and steady reading.

    • Spelling Competitions

      Word games and spelling contests that strengthen vocabulary.

    • Research Projects

      Guided projects that teach pupils to find, check and use information.

    • Literary Activities

      Poetry, drama, creative writing and author-themed events that celebrate literature and give pupils a voice. Dates and details are to be confirmed by the school.

    Getting Started

    How to Use the Library

    Five simple steps from walking in to taking a book home. The school confirms its own procedure.

    1. Search or browse

      Look along the shelves or search for a topic, title or author.

    2. Choose a book

      Pick something that suits your class and your interests.

    3. Ask for help

      The library staff can help you find what you need.

    4. Borrow or read

      Read in the library or borrow, following the school’s procedure.

    5. Return on time

      Bring books back so that others can enjoy them too.

    Guidelines

    Library Rules and Guidelines

    General good habits for any library. The school’s official policies are shown as placeholders until confirmed.

    • Keep the library calm and quiet so everyone can focus.
    • Handle books gently and treat them with care.
    • Return books to the right place or hand them to the library staff.
    • Be kind and respectful to other readers and to staff.
    • Ask for help whenever you need it.
