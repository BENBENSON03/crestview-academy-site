Primary School

Building Strong Foundations for Lifelong Learning

Our primary programme gives pupils a confident start in reading, mathematics, science and creative thinking, in a caring school where every child is known, encouraged and challenged.

Enquire About Admission Explore Our Curriculum

Welcome

Welcome to Our Primary School

The primary years shape how children see themselves as learners. We give every pupil a stable, encouraging classroom where they can grow in confidence, learn good habits and begin to think for themselves.

  • Academically. Strong basics in literacy, numeracy and science, taught clearly and practised often.
  • Socially and emotionally. Respect, teamwork and kindness, with adults who listen.
  • Creatively. Music, art, drama and projects that let pupils express their ideas.
  • With character. Discipline, responsibility and independent thinking that last beyond the classroom.

Academics

Our Academic Curriculum

Seven learning areas that give pupils a broad, balanced education.

  • English Language and Literacy

    Reading with understanding, clear writing, spelling, grammar and confident speaking.

  • Mathematics and Numeracy

    Number sense, operations, shapes, measurement and solving everyday problems.

  • Basic Science and Technology

    Hands-on investigation of living things, materials, energy and the world around us.

  • Social Studies and Civic Education

    Community, culture, citizenship and responsibility, with our own country and the wider world.

  • Computer Studies and Digital Literacy

    Safe, sensible use of computers, typing, simple coding ideas and online safety.

  • Creative Arts and Cultural Education

    Drawing, craft, music, drama and the traditions and stories that shape our culture.

  • Physical and Health Education

    Games, fitness, teamwork and healthy habits for a strong body and mind.

How We Teach

Learning That Is Active and Engaging

  • Interactive Lessons

    Pupils ask, discuss and explain, not just listen.

  • Practical Activities

    Experiments and real objects make ideas concrete.

  • Group Projects

    Working in teams builds cooperation and leadership.

  • Educational Technology

    Digital tools that support, never replace, good teaching.

  • Guided Reading

    Reading in small groups at each pupil's level.

  • Problem-Solving

    Puzzles and challenges that grow critical thinking.

Teachers and Support

Guiding Every Pupil Step by Step

Good teachers notice. Our staff get to know each child's strengths and needs, so support arrives early and encouragement never runs out.

  • Clear Guidance

    Teachers explain, demonstrate and check understanding before moving on.

  • Progress Monitoring

    Regular assessments and feedback show where each pupil is thriving or needs help.

  • Encouraging Participation

    Every voice matters, from the confident speaker to the quiet thinker.

  • Support for Different Needs

    Extra help, extension tasks and flexible approaches so each child can succeed.
